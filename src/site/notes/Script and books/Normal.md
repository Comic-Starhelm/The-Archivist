Case_01
Transcripts regarding the employee, [REDACTED], codename “Normal,” have been uncovered while scouring the ancient library within the cathedral. Many transcripts were recovered and will be posted publicly due to overwhelming inquiries about the hiring and origin of such an infamous colleague.

The first transcript is from a now fired employee asking Prophet a question regarding Normal and his whereabouts.

“So, I assume you’re aware of Normal’s…unique hiring circumstance?”

“I am very aware.”

“May you tell me how he was hired?”

“Well, usually we don’t delve into stuff that could be considered ‘private’, but I can make an exception for Normal’s case.”

“Well…?”

“Well, Normal wasn’t exactly ‘hired’, he was found at a site of interest by a formal member. Their interaction was brief. Normal apparently ‘suplexed that little bitch’, but details are foggy due to Normal being vague and often untruthful regarding his past. Nevertheless, Normal was brought to headquarters by this employee and was introduced to all the other employees. I ordered security to have him executed, but he offered me a proposal: he’d be hired here, actively go out and do dangerous jobs normal people would not do, like interviewing anomalies. He’d take full responsibility for any casualties of team members that he was with during missions, and he would do any job in any division if he was asked to.”

“That’s it? Nothing more…interesting?”

“I can’t tell you ALL the details. Now, don’t you have work to do?”

“Yes, sir.”

More transcripts will be uncovered as we have more documentary members uncover the books in the library. For now, rest with this knowledge on such an oddity in the team.
Case_02
Another transcript was found amongst the piles of books in the library. This one pertains to how Normal got his signature gun. It was the employee Normal calls ‘Lamp Eater’, inquiring about how he got it and why he never lets go of it. The only thing we can’t find is who Lamp Eater was talking to about this matter. It is assumed to be Prophet but no one is sure.

09/20/[REDACTED]:

“So, you’re the infamous ‘Lamp Eater’?”

“Yes.”

“Well, why are you here?”

“Well, I’m curious about why Normal always carries around that gun.”

“Ah, an interesting question. Well, it started with a mission he and Rori took a few months ago.”

“Who’s Rori?”

“Rori is a member of IR who wields a mace they got during that same mission.”

“Continue…”

“Well, they took a mission to a place of interest. I don’t remember the name off the top of my head, but I remember that they were there to find any anomalies that could be harmful or of interest. They said it resembled an ‘amusement park’. Although it looked very…off, they couldn’t quite put their fingers on it but it was different to a normal amusement park. Anyway, they walked around, searching for anomalies, when Normal stumbled across a dunk tank and begged Rori to play it with him. Although, he will say Rori insisted on playing. We believe Rori more. They went to the tank and Rori directly hit the first shot and got to pick out a weapon. Their weapon of choice was a mace. Normal, after a minute of aiming, hit the target and won his weapon, a gun. When they got back, Normal got a year-long suspension for failing the mission. Both weapons were tested, although no records are held for the gun.”

More transcripts will be uncovered and publicized later once the team finds more documents.
